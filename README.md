# BuildWise — Smart Construction Cost Estimation System

BuildWise is a full-stack web-based construction cost estimation and project management platform designed to help construction companies, engineers, and administrators efficiently manage projects, clients, materials, labor rates, BOQs, cost estimations, and project progress.

The system provides role-based access for **Administrators and Engineers**, allowing each user to perform their respective tasks through a centralized and user-friendly platform.

### 🚀 Key Features

- 🔐 Role-Based Authentication for Admin and Engineer
- 👨‍💼 Admin Dashboard & Management
- 👷 Engineer Management
- 🏗️ Construction Type Management
- 🧱 Material & Material Category Management
- 👥 Client Management
- 📋 Project Management
- 💰 Construction Cost Estimation
- 📊 Bill of Quantities (BOQ) Generation
- 👷 Labor Cost Management
- 📈 Project Progress Tracking
- 📄 Estimate & BOQ PDF Generation
- 📚 Estimation History
- 🔎 Project & Data Search
- 👤 Engineer Profile Management
- 📞 Help & Issue Reporting

### 👷 Engineer Workflow

Engineers can view their assigned projects, access client and company information, prepare construction estimates, select materials, calculate quantities and costs, add labor and additional charges, apply GST, generate BOQs, and monitor project progress.

### 🎯 Objective

The primary objective of BuildWise is to digitize and simplify the construction estimation process by reducing manual calculations, improving estimation accuracy, centralizing project information, and helping engineers efficiently manage construction projects from estimation to progress tracking.

BuildWise aims to provide a scalable foundation for modern construction project management and cost estimation.
